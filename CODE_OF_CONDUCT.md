# Community Code of Conduct

CyberSafe welcomes questions, reports and review from people with different experience levels. Communicate respectfully in English or Portuguese and criticize ideas or behavior, not personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific and open to correction.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution and other people's time.
- Distinguish observations from assumptions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Use benign examples; keep security-sensitive details out of public discussion.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, disclosure of private information, malicious attachments and repeated promotional spam are not acceptable. Do not pressure people into disabling protections or testing on their personal data.

## Reporting and enforcement

For public conduct, use GitHub's report-content controls when appropriate, or alert the repository owner without repeating sensitive material. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities. No dedicated private conduct inbox or response SLA is currently promised.

The repository owner may request edits, remove harmful content or restrict participation using GitHub's available moderation controls. Responses should reflect severity, repetition and context, give an explanation where appropriate, and avoid republishing personal details. Participants can request clarification from the owner through an available GitHub contact channel.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests and any project-managed discussion space. They do not establish a staffed moderation team or obligations for unrelated external communities.
